EME 6208: Designing Integrated Media Environments | Fall 2026

This page showcases my image editing project for EME 6208. I created two versions of a book cover titled “Isn’t Graduate School Cool?” to explore how traditional image editing and generative AI can communicate the same idea.

Image Editing

For this project, I designed two versions of a book cover titled Isn’t Graduate School Cool? The title is intentionally playful, while the images reflect the stress and sometimes overwhelming experience of graduate school. I used GIMP to create the first cover and generative AI to produce an alternative interpretation of the same concept.

Book Cover 1: Traditional Editing (GIMP)

Book Cover 2: AI-Generated Design

Project Reflection

Comparing the two approaches helped me think about the creative control, editing decisions, and time involved in producing an image with traditional tools versus generative AI.
